Malware and Cyber Threats

The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

On May 12, 2017, a major cyberattack called WannaCry ransomware began spreading across computers worldwide. This malware locked people's files and demanded money to restore access to them. The attack affected many organizations, including hospitals in the United Kingdom, and caused major disruptions to their services. This event became an important part of cybersecurity history because it showed how quickly malware can spread when computers are not properly updated.

Types of Malware

  1. Spyware - Secretly collects user information.
  2. Worms - Spread between computers without needing user action.

Common Hacker Motives

  1. Stealing personal information to commit fraud.
  2. Disrupting businesses or online services.

Prevention Strategies

  • Regularly update software to fix security problems.
  • Use strong passwords and multi-factor authentication.
